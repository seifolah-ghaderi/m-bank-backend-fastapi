Banking Marketing & Customer Retention Management System
System Overview

The system is an internal CRM and field marketing platform used by a bank to manage marketing campaigns, customer acquisition, customer retention, POS terminal visits, employee performance, and sales verification.

The application is NOT connected to the Core Banking System.

It only manages marketing operations, customer visits, task assignments, campaign execution, verification, and employee scoring.

Whenever bank information is required (such as account number or purchased product), the information is manually entered by employees and later verified by supervisors using another banking system.

The application consists of three user roles:

Supervisor (Administrator)
Branch Manager
Employee (Field Marketer)

The system includes both a web administration panel and a mobile application.

Organizational Structure

The organization consists of:

Cities
Bank Branches
Employees

Each employee belongs to exactly one branch.

Each branch belongs to one city.

Each branch has one manager.

The supervisor has access to the entire system.

Branch managers only manage their own branch.

Employees only access tasks assigned to them.

Master Data

The system manages several master data entities.

These include:

Branches
Cities
Employees
Occupations
Banking Products

Each banking product contains a score value.

The score is used later for employee ranking.

Lead Management

Potential customers (Leads) are imported from Excel files.

Each Lead contains information such as:

First Name
Last Name
Mobile Number
National Code (optional)
Address
Occupation
City
Referrer
Additional Information

Each Lead belongs to a specific branch.

A Lead may participate in multiple marketing campaigns over time.

However,

a Lead cannot belong to more than one ACTIVE campaign simultaneously.

Marketing Campaign

A Supervisor creates marketing campaigns.

Each campaign has:

Title
Start Date
End Date
Status

Possible statuses:

Draft
Active
Closed

The Supervisor filters Leads using different criteria such as:

City
Occupation
Name Search

Selected Leads are automatically assigned to their own branch inside that campaign.

Campaign Execution

Employees open the mobile application and view active campaigns.

Inside a campaign, they see all Leads assigned to their branch.

Employees choose a Lead themselves.

There is no automatic assignment.

Once selected,

the Lead becomes locked for that employee until the process finishes.

Branch Managers may reassign the Lead to another employee if necessary.

Customer Visit

Employees visit the customer.

The visit contains:

Visit Date
Result
Description
Additional Information

Possible results include:

Pending
Interested
Not Interested
No Response

A Lead has one Visit record per Campaign.

If the employee visits again, the same Visit record is updated instead of creating a new Visit.

Product Recommendation

During the visit,

employees may recommend multiple banking products.

If the customer purchases products,

the employee records:

Product
Account Number (or another reference)
Description

A customer cannot purchase the same product multiple times.

Each purchased product requires independent verification.

Sales Verification

Every purchased product enters a verification workflow.

A Supervisor manually validates the information against another banking system.

Verification result:

Approved
Rejected

If approved:

Product sale becomes final.
Employee receives product score.
Customer enters the retention process.

If rejected:

The record returns to the employee.
Employee edits the information.
Employee resubmits the verification.

Rejected records never receive scores.

Customer Conversion

After successful verification,

the Lead becomes a Bank Customer.

The original Lead information must remain available for historical purposes.

Customer retention is based on verified customers only.

Customer Retention

The system automatically creates Retention Tasks after a customer joins the bank.

Retention schedules are configurable.

Examples:

15 days
45 days
90 days

Retention Tasks may require Supervisor approval before publication.

Each task is automatically assigned to the employee who successfully acquired the customer.

Employees perform the visit.

Record the result.

Archive the task.

Branch Managers may reassign retention tasks if necessary.

POS Device Management

The system manages POS terminals.

POS devices are imported from external files.

Each device contains information such as:

Device Number
Owner
Address
Branch
Status

A POS device belongs to one owner at a time.

A device may move between branches over time.

POS Transaction History

Monthly transaction summaries are imported.

Each monthly record contains:

Device
From Date
To Date
Transaction Amount

The system calculates device productivity dynamically using imported history.

The productivity formula is outside the scope of this phase.

POS Visit Workflow

Branch Managers assign POS visit tasks to employees.

Employees visit the device.

They register:

Visit Result
Tags
Description

Possible tags:

Broken Device
New Service Requested
Maintenance Required

Completed visits are archived.

Employee Scoring

Each banking product has a predefined score.

Only verified sales generate scores.

Rejected sales generate no score.

Scores are accumulated per employee.

League System

The system contains two independent ranking systems.

Marketing League

Calculated from verified product scores.

Each league has:

Start Date
End Date

Employees are ranked based on accumulated scores.

Retention League

Calculated using customer retention performance.

Retention League is always available but may also operate within configurable time periods.

Important Business Constraints
A Lead may belong to multiple campaigns over time but only one Active campaign simultaneously.
Employees manually pick Leads from their branch.
A selected Lead remains assigned until completion.
Branch Managers may reassign tasks.
One Visit record exists per Lead per Campaign.
Multiple products may be registered in a single Visit.
Each purchased product has its own verification.
Verification is mandatory before scoring.
Rejected verification returns to the employee.
Customer Retention begins only after successful verification.
Retention tasks are generated automatically.
POS devices are imported.
Monthly POS transactions are imported separately.
The system is independent from the Core Banking System.
Historical data must never be physically delete
