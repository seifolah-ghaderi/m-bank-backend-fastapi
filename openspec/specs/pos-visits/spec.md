# pos-visits Specification

## Purpose

Route Supervisor-selected terminals to their owning branches and preserve why employees were asked to visit them.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Supervisor dispatch
Only Supervisors SHALL create follow-up work by selecting terminal IDs and a performance period. Dispatch SHALL route each non-deleted, non-retired terminal to its current active branch with status `pending_assignment`, selector identity/time, and no employee assignee. Low, normal, high, and no-data terminals SHALL be selectable. Bulk dispatch SHALL accept at most 200 terminal IDs and return a result per terminal.

#### Scenario: Dispatch to owning branches
- **WHEN** the Supervisor selects eligible terminals from several branches
- **THEN** each branch receives only its own terminal's pending work and no employee task exists until a manager assigns it.

#### Scenario: Manager creates independent work
- **WHEN** a manager or employee attempts dispatch or creates a visit directly from a terminal
- **THEN** the response is 403 and no work is created.

### Requirement: Immutable selection evidence
Dispatch SHALL compute and save the selected period, amount/count totals or nulls, no-data flag, classification, lower/upper thresholds, rial unit, and method alongside each task. Evidence SHALL be generated by the server from a consistent database view and remain unchanged by assignment, correction, transfer, later imports, or policy edits.

#### Scenario: Later correction and policy change
- **WHEN** transactions or global thresholds change after dispatch
- **THEN** task detail shows the original snapshot and live performance queries reflect the new data/policy separately.

#### Scenario: Client-supplied score
- **WHEN** a client attempts to supply or overwrite selection totals/classification
- **THEN** the request cannot replace server-calculated immutable evidence.

### Requirement: Branch queue assignment
Only the owning branch's current manager SHALL assign pending work to an active employee of that same active branch. Assignment SHALL set `assigned_to`, `assigned_by`, and `assigned_at`, optional non-past due date, and instructions up to 2,000 characters, transitioning to `assigned`. Managers SHALL NOT assign work from another branch or create work absent a Supervisor dispatch.

#### Scenario: Assign selected work
- **WHEN** the manager assigns a pending task to an eligible employee
- **THEN** it becomes assigned, appears in that employee's task list, and retains the Supervisor selection snapshot.

#### Scenario: Queue with no manager
- **WHEN** a Supervisor dispatches to an active branch currently without a manager
- **THEN** the work remains pending and is available to the branch's eligible manager when one is appointed.

### Requirement: One open follow-up per terminal
A terminal SHALL have at most one open follow-up across `pending_assignment` and `assigned`. Duplicate dispatch SHALL fail with 409 `device_has_open_visit`. Completed or cancelled work SHALL NOT block later dispatch. Concurrency SHALL NOT produce duplicate open work, cross-branch assignments, or successful completion after transfer cancellation.

#### Scenario: Concurrent dispatch
- **WHEN** two requests dispatch the same terminal concurrently
- **THEN** at most one open task is created and the other gets the domain conflict.

### Requirement: Reassignment and cancellation
Owning branch managers SHALL reassign assigned work only to eligible employees in their branch. Supervisors or owning managers SHALL cancel pending/assigned work with a reason up to 255 characters. Transfer SHALL automatically cancel both open states with `device_transferred`. Closed work SHALL reject further assignment, reassignment, cancellation, or completion with `visit_archived`.

#### Scenario: Reassign within branch
- **WHEN** a manager reassigns an assigned task
- **THEN** the former employee loses access, the new employee gains access, both receive their relevant notifications, and evidence is unchanged.

### Requirement: Employee completion and Supervisor correction
Only the assigned employee SHALL complete an assigned visit with result `visited`, `owner_unavailable`, `shop_closed`, or `device_not_found`, unique active tag codes, optional description up to 2,000 characters, and a non-future visit time no earlier than assignment. Completion SHALL archive the task with completion time. Only Supervisors SHALL correct completed result/tags/description, with audit and no snapshot edits.

#### Scenario: Another employee submits completion
- **WHEN** an employee attempts to complete another employee's visit
- **THEN** the response is 404 and no visit state changes.

#### Scenario: Complete with active tags
- **WHEN** the assignee submits a valid completion
- **THEN** result/tags persist, the assigning manager is notified, and terminal status remains unchanged.

### Requirement: Visit visibility and tag administration
Supervisors SHALL see all work; managers SHALL see their branch queue and visits; employees SHALL see only their own assigned or historical visits. Employee lists SHALL default to assigned work ordered by due date with nulls last. Lists SHALL support state, archive, overdue, assignee, terminal, branch, result, tag, and date filters. Only Supervisors SHALL administer tags; all roles SHALL list active tags.

#### Scenario: Employee pending queue request
- **WHEN** an employee lists visits while the branch has unassigned work
- **THEN** those pending tasks and other employees' tasks are absent, including their counts.

#### Scenario: Seed tags
- **WHEN** the initial POS tag catalog is available
- **THEN** it includes `broken_device`, `new_service_requested`, and `maintenance_required`; deactivation preserves historical links.
